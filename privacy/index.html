Sujood Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Android package com.sujoodlock.app

Sujood (“the app”) is a prayer times, Qibla and prayer-focus app. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. Almost everything it does happens on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app uses, what leaves your device and why.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, and no sale of data.
  • Prayer times, the Qibla direction, your prayer log, streaks, the app lock and the widgets are calculated and stored only on your device.
  • The app sends a small amount of data off the device only for: anonymous usage statistics (which you can turn off), searching for a city, and finding nearby mosques.

Data that stays on your device

DataWhy it is used
Location (precise or approximate, your choice)To calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. These are worked out on the device.
Your prayer log, streaks, Qada counts, settingsTo show your progress. Stored in the app’s private storage on your device.
The name of the app on screen (Android Accessibility service, only if you turn the app lock on)To pause the apps you chose at prayer time. The app only reads which app is in the foreground. It never reads screen content, messages, passwords or what you type, and this information never leaves your device.
App usage access (Android, optional fallback for the app lock)The same purpose as above, for people who choose not to enable the Accessibility service. It stays on the device.
The list of installed appsSo you can choose which apps to pause. It stays on the device.
NotificationsTo remind you at each prayer time. They are scheduled on the device.

You can remove all of this data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Data that leaves your device

WhatSent toWhenWhy
Anonymous usage statistics: which screens are opened, when the app is opened or closed, the app version, device model and operating system version, and a random identifier that is not linked to youPostHog (analytics provider)While you use the app, unless you turn it offTo understand which features are used and to improve the app. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
The city name you typeOpen-Meteo geocoding serviceOnly when you search for a cityTo find the city’s coordinates for prayer times.
Your approximate location (coordinates)OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim and Overpass)Only when you open “Nearby mosques”To list mosques near you. Results are cached on the device.
A mosque’s locationGoogle Maps (or your maps app)Only when you tap “Directions”To open directions. That app’s own privacy policy applies.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, messages or any account details. We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share data for advertising.

Permissions

  • Location: prayer times and Qibla (on the device), and nearby mosques (only when you open it).
  • Notifications: adhan reminders and the optional prayer countdown.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so reminders and app locks start exactly at prayer time.
  • Accessibility service (optional, app lock only): to detect which app is open, as described above. You can turn it off at any time in your phone’s Accessibility settings.
  • Usage access and display over other apps (optional, app lock fallback only).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Data on your device stays in the app’s private storage and is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Anonymous usage statistics are kept by PostHog under its data retention policy and are not linked to your identity.
